title: Booked Guard
description: >-
  Single-screen web app for coordinating family shifts by day. A carousel of 30
  past and 30 future days shows three fixed shifts (day, afternoon and night)
  with editable hours and one person assigned per shift; each day is marked as
  covered, partially covered or uncovered. It includes a people panel, a change
  log and real-time sync across devices. No login: it is shared through a
  private, trusted link.
